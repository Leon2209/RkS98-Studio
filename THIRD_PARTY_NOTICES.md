PySide6/Qt, HIDAPI, and development tools retain their own licenses. Installing
or redistributing this project does not replace those license obligations.

Application bundles include PySide6/Qt under the LGPLv3 option, with the LGPLv3
and GPLv3 license texts and dependency metadata. Their source is available from
[Qt source archives](https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/) and
[Qt for Python sources](https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/).

6.8.3; local builds may differ. You may extract the native package to replace its
dynamic Qt libraries and rebuild the application; there is no signature lock.
